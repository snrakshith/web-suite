## February Worklog

**Project:** Astrum Engineering – Mobile Responsive Web App

### **Overview:**

This month, I worked on developing a mobile-responsive web app for **Astrum Engineering**, showcasing key pages of the company’s online presence. The web app focuses on providing a user-friendly experience across all devices, with special attention given to mobile responsiveness. The following pages were created for the initial release:

1. **Home Page**
2. **About Us Page**
3. **Contact Us Page**

---

### **1. Home Page:**

- **Objective:** The home page serves as the first point of contact for visitors and provides an overview of Astrum Engineering’s services, values, and expertise.
- **Features Developed:**
  - **Header:** Includes a logo, navigation menu, and a call-to-action (CTA) button.
  - **Hero Section:** Large, impactful image of engineering projects with a brief welcome message.
  - **Overview of Services:** A brief description of the services offered, with links to more details.
  - **Mobile Responsiveness:** Ensured that the layout adjusts seamlessly on mobile devices, including the reflow of text and resizing of images for readability.

**Challenges:**

- Optimizing the hero image for both desktop and mobile without affecting load times or visual quality.
- Maintaining clean typography and proper alignment across different screen sizes.

---

### **2. About Us Page:**

- **Objective:** The About Us page provides visitors with a background on Astrum Engineering, its mission, and core values.
- **Features Developed:**
  - **Company Mission & Values:** Section explaining the company’s goals and commitment to quality engineering.
  - **Team Showcase:** Brief introductions to key team members, along with photos and roles.
  - **Mobile Responsiveness:** Content dynamically adjusts, with the layout stacking for smaller screens to ensure easy reading.

**Challenges:**

- Presenting large amounts of text in a way that remains easy to navigate on smaller devices.
- Integrating team member profiles in a visually appealing and space-efficient manner on mobile screens.

---

### **3. Contact Us Page:**

- **Objective:** The contact page allows visitors to get in touch with Astrum Engineering, either through direct inquiries or by using a contact form.
- **Features Developed:**
  - **Contact Form:** A simple form allowing users to submit inquiries with fields for name, email, subject, and message.
  - **Location Map:** Integration of an interactive map showing the office location.
  - **Social Media Links:** Quick access to Astrum Engineering’s social media profiles.
  - **Mobile Responsiveness:** The contact form adjusts dynamically for smaller screens, ensuring ease of use on mobile devices.

**Challenges:**

- Ensuring that the contact form is functional across various screen sizes, particularly on mobile devices.
- Integrating a responsive map that works on both desktop and mobile, with touch-friendly interactions.

---

### **Technologies Used:**

- **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript:** For the foundational structure, styling, and interactivity of the pages.
- **TailwindCSS:** Utilized for creating a responsive grid layout and ensuring mobile compatibility.
- **Responsive Design Principles:** Ensured all pages adapt fluidly across devices, with particular attention to mobile-first design.
